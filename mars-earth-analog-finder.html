4 · Limitations

  • Mars terrain, elevation, ice access and flatness are interpolated from only 25 hand-compiled reference regions, so values between regions are estimates.
  • Mars temperature uses a simple latitude and elevation formula, not a climate model. Night lows are a rough estimate.
  • The suitability score (hab()) and the matching weights are heuristic choices, not an established scientific index. Use the sliders to see how rankings change.
  • The 23 Earth sites use approximate literature values; other Earth points are interpolated from the nearest sites and a latitude climate envelope.
  • The calibration check in 3A compares against 7 sites whose reference values were entered by hand and used to tune the model, so it shows agreement with calibration points, not independent validation.
  • Intended use: first-pass screening of candidate analog sites, to be followed by checks against the original datasets.
